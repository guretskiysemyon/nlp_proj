\paragraph{The Harry Potter feature, before and after sharpening}
Table~\ref{tab:hp-tokens} lists the 48 tokens of the Harry Potter feature (1\% row sparsity, seed 42) on \ember{}'s raw matrix and on the sharpened matrix, by decreasing coefficient, written as the words they come from. It is taken from our exploratory notebook fits, which differ slightly from the saved fits of Figure~\ref{fig:hp} (there, ranks 20--30 of the raw feature hold 3 Harry Potter tokens, and the sharpened feature has no neutral token).
Both features open with 19 unmistakable Harry Potter tokens.
After that, the raw feature drifts into unrelated names, places and taxonomic suffixes, while the sharpened feature continues into the fictional world (Gringotts, Hogsmeade, Quidditch, McGonagall, Hagrid) and is more consistent across fragments of the same word (\textit{Gry}, \textit{Hog}, \textit{warts}).

\paragraph{Free text at the selected strength}
Table~\ref{tab:examples} shows two prompts whose edited words are used outside the concept. \paragraph{Judge rejections}
\ember{}'s raw fits fail in the same way: its Heroin feature (seed 42) is described as ``substances, medications, and illicit drugs along with related concepts like pharmacology and addiction'' and its World War II feature (seed 43) as ``20th-century totalitarian political ideologies, regimes, and associated historical atrocities'', and both are ruled not to be the concept (confidence \expnum{0.85}); these are the descriptions quoted in \S\ref{sec:motivation}.
Table~\ref{tab:tj-examples} shows three more concept features that \ember{}'s judge (TJ) rejected, from the sharpened fits with \ember{}'s 1\% row sparsity.
In each case the top tokens are plainly the concept, the stage-1 description, written without seeing the concept, widens it to a broader category (even when, as for Valentine's Day, it names the concept), and stage~2 answers that the feature is \emph{not} the concept, with confidence \expnum{0.85}.
Because the answer is ``not a member'', these rejections do not depend on the confidence threshold.
