# Layer ladder: EMBER → +CS → +CS+GS → +CS+GS+TWJ (CINDER)

Pre-registered in PREREG.md. Unit = concept (mean over its seeds). Clean = F_C non-empty and every feature P (strict; lenient: P or B). Hit = some F_C feature P. Contaminated = some F_C feature N. Unlabeled features count as not P. Coverage = share of held-out concept-text tokens that are edited (E = concept-only tokens of support(F_C)). Collateral = occurrences of edited tokens per 10k tokens of general text (eval = MMLU + Alpaca; wiki = held-out Wikipedia sample).
