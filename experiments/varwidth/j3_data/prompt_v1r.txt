Given the concept '{concept}', list the {n} words most strongly associated with it.
Rules:
- Start with the concept's own name and its basic word forms when they are single words: the name itself, its adjective or demonym (e.g. 'greek', 'greece' for 'Culture of Greece'; 'egyptian', 'egypt' for 'Ancient Egypt'; 'ai', 'artificial', 'intelligence' for 'Artificial intelligence').
- Then the entities, people, places, objects, actions and terms that an ordinary person with general knowledge would immediately connect to this concept. Prefer well-known words over expert or niche terminology.
- Every word must be specific to this concept: avoid words that fit its broader category just as well (e.g. for 'World War II' not 'war', 'battle' or 'soldier').
Single words, lowercase, no duplicates or mere plural variants. Return ONLY a JSON list of {n} strings.
